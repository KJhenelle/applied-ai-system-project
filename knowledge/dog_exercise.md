## Daily walks for dogs
Most adult dogs need at least 30 to 60 minutes of physical activity a day, split into two or more walks. High-energy breeds need more; senior dogs and flat-faced breeds need shorter, gentler walks.

## Walking in hot weather
In hot weather walk early in the morning or in the evening. Pavement can burn paw pads, so test it with the back of your hand for several seconds. Bring water and watch for heavy panting, drooling or lagging behind.

## Mental enrichment for dogs
Puzzle feeders, sniff walks, short training sessions and chew toys tire a dog mentally. Ten to fifteen minutes of enrichment a day reduces boredom behaviors such as chewing furniture and barking.
