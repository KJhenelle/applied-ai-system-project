## Brushing and bathing dogs
Brush short-coated dogs weekly and long-coated dogs several times a week to prevent mats. Most dogs only need a bath every one to three months, since frequent bathing dries out the skin. Use a shampoo made for dogs, never human shampoo.

## Nail and ear care
Trim nails every three to four weeks so they do not click on the floor. Check ears weekly for redness, odor or discharge, which can signal an infection that needs a veterinarian.

## Dental care
Brush teeth several times a week with pet toothpaste. Never use human toothpaste, which can contain xylitol. Bad breath or red gums can signal dental disease.
