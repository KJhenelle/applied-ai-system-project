## Giving medication on schedule
Give medication at the same time each day and set a reminder, because consistent timing keeps drug levels steady. Never skip or double a dose without asking the veterinarian. If a dose is missed, call the clinic for guidance rather than guessing.

## Heartworm and flea prevention
Monthly heartworm and flea preventatives work best when given on the same date every month. Mark the date on a calendar and record each dose so a missed month is noticed quickly.

## Hiding pills safely
Pills can be hidden in a small piece of soft food or a pill pocket, but ask the veterinarian first, because some medications must be given on an empty stomach or must not be crushed.
