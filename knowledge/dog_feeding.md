## Feeding schedule for adult dogs
Most adult dogs do well with two measured meals a day, roughly 8 to 12 hours apart. A consistent feeding time helps digestion and house training. Puppies usually need three to four smaller meals a day until about six months old.

## Water and meal timing
Fresh water should always be available. Avoid vigorous exercise for about an hour before and after a large meal, especially for deep-chested breeds, because of the risk of bloat.

## Foods that are dangerous for dogs
Chocolate, grapes, raisins, onions, garlic, xylitol (a sweetener found in some gum and peanut butter), macadamia nuts and alcohol are toxic to dogs. If a dog eats any of these, call a veterinarian or a pet poison hotline immediately instead of waiting for symptoms.
