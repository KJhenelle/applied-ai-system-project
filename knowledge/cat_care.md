## Feeding schedule for adult cats
Adult cats usually eat two or more small meals a day at consistent times. Cats should always have fresh water, and wet food adds useful moisture to their diet. Sudden changes in appetite that last more than a day deserve a veterinary check.

## Foods that are dangerous for cats
Lilies, onions, garlic, chocolate, grapes, raisins, alcohol and caffeine are toxic to cats. Lilies are especially dangerous: even a small amount of pollen or vase water can cause kidney failure. Contact a veterinarian right away after any exposure.

## Play and enrichment for cats
Cats benefit from two or three short play sessions a day, around ten to fifteen minutes each, using wand toys or chase toys. Playing before a meal imitates the hunt, eat, groom, sleep cycle and can improve sleep at night.

## Grooming cats
Short-haired cats usually need brushing about once a week, long-haired cats several times a week. Regular brushing reduces hairballs. Trim claws every two to three weeks if they do not wear down naturally.
